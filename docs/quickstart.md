# Quickstart

Search the tables by plasmid accession or gene name. You can also [download the archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19885239) and [analyse the data locally](tutorial.md).

For example, plasmid [CP029718.1](https://ccb-microbe.cs.uni-saarland.de/plsdb2025/plasmid?NUCCORE_ACC=CP029718.1) carries two complete integrons. Its gene annotations include *bla*<sub>VIM-1</sub>.
